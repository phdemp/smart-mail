What RPM & RPD mean
RPM (Requests Per Minute): caps how many API calls this provider gets in any 60‑second window. Enforced client‑side by a token bucket — if full, the request queues up to 2s (regen) or 30s (classify), then falls through to the next provider.
RPD (Requests Per Day): total allowed calls in 24 h, resets at midnight UTC. Tracked in the local DB. When exhausted, the router skips this provider with skipped_quota and tries the next one.
Leave blank to use the provider's default ceiling. Free tiers today: Groq ≈ 30/min & ~14 400/day (but watch the 12 k tokens‑per‑minute cap on 70b); Gemini 2.5 Flash ≈ 10/min & ~500/day.
