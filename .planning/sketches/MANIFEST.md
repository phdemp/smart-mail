# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction

Neomorphism white — a soft, tactile UI built entirely on a single #f0f0f0 background with double box-shadows (`6px 6px 12px #d1d1d1, -6px -6px 12px #ffffff`) to create depth without borders or color fills. Interactive controls are convex (raised), inputs and display areas are concave (inset). The aesthetic is calm and three-dimensional — emails feel like physical objects you pick up and read, not information on a flat screen.

Locked from CONTEXT.md (D-UI-01): this direction is not re-evaluated in sketches, only applied.

## Reference Points

- Neumorphism.io design patterns (canonical double-shadow recipe)
- Apple Mail on macOS Sonoma (panel depth hierarchy reference)
- Craft app (spatial metaphor: content "rises" when selected)
